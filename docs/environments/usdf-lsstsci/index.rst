##############################################################
usdf-lsstsci — usdf-rsp-lsstsci.slac.stanford.edu (LSSTsci RSP)
##############################################################

``usdf-lsstsci`` is the production environment for the Rubin Science Platform at the United States Data Facility (USDF) hosted at SLAC.
``usdf-lsstsci`` is used by project staff for processing, calibration, testing, and other project activities.
